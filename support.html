Support

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Lumi runs entirely on your device, which means most fixes belong to you: open the item and change it. Below are the answers to the things people ask most.

Getting Started

How do I add something?

Just write it in the chat, as a full sentence. Lumi reads the sentence and files the item for you.

Try: Call the dentist tomorrow at 10am
Try: Every Monday swim for 45 minutes
Try: I want to build a project called Renovation, with steps research, budget, and book a contractor

How does Lumi decide where something goes?

It reads the date out of your sentence. If it finds a date or a clear deadline, the item is scheduled. If it doesn't, the item is held without a date and you decide when it matters.

What's the difference between the panels?

  • Today — what you've asked for today, plus a short list of things that came due.
  • Upcoming — everything with a date in the future, in order.
  • Anytime — things worth doing, but with no date attached.
  • Projects — work that has an outcome, with the steps under it.

Common Questions

Lumi put something in the wrong place. How do I fix it?

Open the item and change it. Swipe left on it for quick moves, or press and hold the card for the full menu. If the App did something you didn't want, tap the Undo bar right after — every action it takes can be reversed.

It didn't understand what I wrote.

Lumi will say so rather than guess. It then offers two ways to rephrase it. Writing a shorter sentence with the date separated out usually helps most: Buy QQQ on the 20th is easier to read than Remind me to buy QQQ on the 20th of every three months.

My reminders aren't arriving.

Check, in order:

  • iOS Settings > Notifications > Lumi — make sure notifications are allowed.
  • Focus modes — a Do Not Disturb or Sleep Focus will hold reminders until it ends.
  • In Lumi — confirm the item still has the date and reminder you expect. An all-day item fires that morning; an item with a clock time fires shortly before it.

Why isn't everything I write being added?

Lumi only creates items it can read clearly. It has no guessing and no second chances — if a sentence is too vague, it asks instead of inventing. Try naming the thing and the date.

How do I sync to my other device?

Turn on iCloud sync in Settings, and sign both devices into the same Apple Account. Lumi only asks for iCloud when you turn this on yourself.

Where is my data? Can you see it?

On your device, and in your own iCloud account if you enabled sync. Nowhere else. We have no servers and receive nothing. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up or move my data?

Use iCloud sync if you have another device, or export your metrics from Settings. Because Lumi stores your tasks locally, there is no server-side copy we can send you.

Troubleshooting

An item disappeared

Check Today and Upcoming, then the Trash via the Lists menu. Items you complete go to the Logbook, and items you delete are recoverable from Trash until you empty it.

The App didn't ask for a date

That means it read your sentence as a complete item with no date. If you wanted one, add it: Pay rent on the 1st. If you wanted an open-ended item, Renew my passport is correct as written.

Everything is in Anytime and nothing is scheduled

Lumi schedules what it can read a date from. Add dates to the items that have deadlines, and they'll move themselves into Upcoming.
