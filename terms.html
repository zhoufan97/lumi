2. What Lumi Is

Lumi is a personal task and project assistant for iPhone and iPad. It lets you:

  • Capture tasks, projects, notes, and deadlines by typing or speaking them into a conversation
  • Let the App file each item into Today, Upcoming, Anytime, or Projects on its own
  • Set reminders, including repeating ones, delivered as notifications on your device
  • Keep a written conversation with the App that you can search later
  • Optionally sync across your own devices through your personal iCloud account

The App's language understanding runs entirely on your device. It does not use a remote AI service.
